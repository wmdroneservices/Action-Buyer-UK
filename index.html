HOW IT WORKS

Clear from the
first click.

We keep the journey focused: tell us about your equipment, receive an initial valuation where our system supports it, then let our team complete the review and final offer.

Tell us about your gear

Choose the equipment and give us the details that affect its value, including model, package and condition.

Receive your initial valuation

Where the valuation system supports the item, the equipment details and condition are used to calculate an initial valuation.

We review every submission

Your submission is checked by our team. Where the item, condition or circumstances require it, the valuation is completed manually.

See your final offer

Your account keeps the valuation and offer together, so you can review the figure and decide whether you want to proceed.

Send your gear and get paid

If you accept, we'll guide you through shipping and inspection. Once the final value is confirmed, payment is arranged.
