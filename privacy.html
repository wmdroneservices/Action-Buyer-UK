Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use and protect it.

Information We Collect

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Full postal address
  • Drone serial number
  • Controller serial number
  • Flight information (hours)
  • Battery information (cycles)
  • Uploaded photographs
  • Quote information

Why We Collect This Information

Your personal and equipment information is collected to provide accurate purchase quotes, process your sale, conduct ownership and fraud checks, arrange shipping and payment, and deliver good customer service.

Quote and Purchase Processing

We use your data to generate instant quotes, verify the condition and ownership of your drone, and finalize the sale including payment or returns.

Shipping and Returns

Your address is needed to send shipping instructions and return equipment if necessary.

Fraud and Ownership Checks

Serial numbers and account binding status may be checked to prevent fraudulent sales.

Payment Processing

Payment details are handled securely. This front-end prototype does not process payments—backend integration is required.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. This prototype does not include backend storage—backend integration is required for secure storage.

Data Retention

Your data will be retained only as long as necessary to complete your sale and for legal compliance.
