2. Valuations

You submit information about your equipment, including its model, condition, accessories, faults and any other information requested during the valuation process.

Our team reviews the information and photographs supplied and may make an offer for individual items or for a complete package where appropriate.

A valuation is not a guarantee that the equipment will ultimately be purchased for that amount. The equipment must be received and inspected before the final purchase price is confirmed.

3. Manual Valuations and Offers

GearCashOut may request additional information or photographs before preparing an offer.

Where items are valued separately, you may be able to accept or refuse individual offers. Where a complete-package offer is made, the package may be accepted as a whole.

All offers are subject to inspection and verification unless we expressly confirm otherwise.

4. Inspection and Verification

When equipment is received, we may inspect and test it to verify the information supplied during the valuation process.

Depending on the equipment, checks may include model, serial number, functionality, condition, damage, accessories, battery condition or cycle count, usage information, account or activation status and package contents.

For DJI drones, we may also check relevant flight information and whether the drone is unbound from the seller's DJI account.

5. Revised Offers

If the equipment differs materially from the information supplied, or if inspection identifies a fault, missing item or other factor that affects its value, we may make a revised offer.

Where a revised offer is made, you will be given the opportunity to accept or reject it before the transaction is completed.

If you reject a revised offer, we will arrange for the equipment to be returned to the return address you supplied, subject to any applicable delivery arrangements communicated to you.

We may check serial numbers or other identifying information where reasonably necessary to verify ownership or the status of equipment.

If ownership is uncertain, we may pause or refuse the transaction and may request reasonable evidence of ownership.

You must disclose known faults, damage, modifications and other material issues that could affect the value or safe operation of the equipment.
