Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Instant Quote work?
You select your drone model, package, condition, flight time, batteries, and upload photos. The system calculates an instant purchase price based on your inputs.
Is the Instant Quote final?
No. It is based on the information and photographs you provide. A physical inspection is done when received and the final offer may differ.
What happens when you receive my drone?
We inspect the drone verifying model, serial numbers, flight time, battery cycles, condition, damage, package contents, and unbound status.
What if the final valuation is lower?
You can accept the revised offer or reject it. If rejected, your equipment will be returned to your provided address.
What happens if I reject the final offer?
Your equipment is returned to the full return address you supplied during the process.
Do you buy damaged drones?
Yes. We consider damaged and non-working drones for refurbishment or spare parts.
Do you buy drones that don't work?
Yes. Even drones that don't work may have value for parts.
Do you buy controllers separately?
Yes, we buy controllers separately and include them in our valuation.
Do you buy batteries separately?
Yes, batteries are valued individually based on type and cycle count.
Do you buy older DJI drones?
Yes, we buy older drones at appropriate low prices.
Do you buy Mini 2?
Yes, Mini 2 and other models are included in our pricing catalogue.
Do you buy very low-hour drones?
Yes, but low flight time does not increase price above the base price.
Do you buy factory-sealed drones?
Yes, factory-sealed drones qualify for the base purchase price, subject to verification.
Why do you need flight-time information?
Flight time affects drone value due to wear and tear and maintenance requirements.
Why do you need battery-cycle information?
Batteries lose capacity over cycles; we adjust valuation based on this information.
Why do you need an unbound screenshot?
To ensure the drone is not linked to a DJI account, which affects the ability to resell.
Why do you need my full address?
To return equipment if you reject the final valuation and for shipping verification.
How do I send my drone?
After accepting the instant quote, you will receive shipping instructions and a printable label (backend integration required).
