WORKFLOW BOUNDARY

Where the two sections meet

Purchase Inventory is the handover point. Items remain in the purchasing workflow while they are being received, inspected, tested and prepared. When staff use Send to Sales, the item leaves Purchase Inventory and becomes available in the Sales Dashboard.
