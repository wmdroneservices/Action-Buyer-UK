How It Works

STEP 1: GET AN INSTANT QUOTE

Select your drone, package and condition and provide the requested information.

STEP 2: SEND YOUR EQUIPMENT

Once you accept the Instant Quote, follow the shipping instructions provided.

STEP 3: WE INSPECT IT

We check the equipment against the information and photographs supplied. We verify:

  • Model
  • Package
  • Serial number
  • Flight hours
  • Battery cycles
  • Condition
  • Damage
  • Package contents
  • Unbound status

STEP 4: GET PAID

If everything matches, the quoted price is confirmed.

If the inspection results in a different valuation, the customer can accept or reject the revised final offer.

If the customer rejects the revised offer, the equipment is returned to the full address supplied by the customer.
