A clear route from gear to cash.

We combine a structured valuation system with human review, so the information you provide is used consistently and every submission is checked before the final offer.

Tell us about your equipment

Select your equipment, package, condition and the other information we need, then submit your valuation.

Receive your initial valuation

Where supported, our valuation system calculates an initial value using the equipment details, package and condition you provide.

Expert review

Every submission is reviewed by our team. Where the equipment, condition, completeness or other circumstances require it, the valuation is completed manually.

Review your final offer

Your account shows the valuation and offer so you can decide whether you want to proceed.

Send your equipment and get paid

If you accept an offer, follow the shipping instructions provided in your customer account. When we receive your equipment, we verify the submitted details before confirming the final value.

  • Model
  • Package
  • Serial number
  • Flight hours where applicable
  • Battery cycles where applicable
  • Condition
  • Damage
  • Package contents
  • Unbound status where applicable
