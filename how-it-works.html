How It Works

STEP 1: SUBMIT YOUR EQUIPMENT

Select your equipment, package, condition and the other information we need, then submit your valuation request.

STEP 2: AUTOMATIC VALUATION

Where supported, our valuation system provides an automatic valuation using the details you supply.

STEP 3: MANUAL VALUATION

Your submission can then receive a manual valuation where required. Our team reviews the information and photographs and prepares the appropriate offer.

STEP 4: SEND YOUR EQUIPMENT

If you accept an offer, follow the shipping instructions provided in your customer account and by email. When we receive your equipment, we verify the submitted details before confirming the final value.

  • Model
  • Package
  • Serial number
  • Flight hours
  • Battery cycles
  • Condition
  • Damage
  • Package contents
  • Unbound status

STEP 5: GET PAID

Once the required checks are complete, the final value is confirmed.

If the checks result in a different valuation, you can accept or reject the revised final offer.

If you reject the revised offer, the equipment is returned to the full address supplied by you.
