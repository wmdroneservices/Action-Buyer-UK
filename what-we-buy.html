Equipment worth buying. Gear worth moving on.

We buy a range of drones, cameras and photography, video and audio equipment. We consider new, used, older and selected damaged equipment, subject to condition, completeness and resale value.

How valuation works: Our system can calculate an initial valuation for supported equipment from the details and condition you provide. Every submission is then reviewed by our team before the final offer is confirmed.

DRONES

DJI and other recognised drone brands, including consumer, FPV, commercial and professional equipment.

We can also consider controllers, batteries, chargers and selected accessories.

ACTION CAMERAS

GoPro, Insta360, DJI Action and other recognised action cameras and related accessories.

CAMERAS

Digital, mirrorless, DSLR and compact cameras from recognised manufacturers.

LENSES & ACCESSORIES

Camera lenses, flashes, filters and selected photography accessories.

VIDEO EQUIPMENT

Gimbals, monitors, lighting, tripods and other suitable video-production equipment.

AUDIO EQUIPMENT

Microphones, wireless microphone systems, audio recorders and selected recording equipment.

WHAT CONDITION DO WE ACCEPT?

We consider equipment in a range of conditions, including used equipment, older models, factory-sealed items and selected damaged or non-working equipment.

Value depends on the model, age, condition, completeness, functionality and current demand. Equipment must be lawfully owned and available for sale.

HOW WE VALUE YOUR EQUIPMENT

Initial valuation: Where the system supports the equipment, your model, package and condition are used to calculate an initial value.

Expert review: Every submission is reviewed by our team. Where further judgement is required, the valuation is completed manually before the final offer is confirmed.
